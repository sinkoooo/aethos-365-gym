Radio Configuration - eNodeB 8820

How to test with Chrome Extension:
  1. Load the 'extension' folder in Chrome as an unpacked extension.
  2. Open this HTML file in your browser.
  3. Click the Extension popup and hit 'Start Mapping Mode'.
  4. Click on the "Frequency Band Indicator" input box and the "Apply Configuration" button. (They will flash red).
  5. Open the Extension popup again, hit 'Start Chat Mode', and ask something like "Where is the apply button?" or "Where is the frequency band?"
  6. The extension will guide you!
